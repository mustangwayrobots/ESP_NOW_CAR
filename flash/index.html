Choose your board

Plug it in

Connect the board to this computer with a USB-C data cable. Close Arduino IDE's Serial Monitor if it's open.

3

Install

Click install, pick the board's port in the pop-up, and wait for it to finish. Your saved pairing and settings are kept.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Please open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop computer.
USB access needs a secure page. Open this page from its https:// GitHub Pages address.
Having trouble?
  • No port in the list? Try another cable. Many USB cables only charge.
  • Still nothing? Hold the BOOT button, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again.
  • After it finishes, press RESET or unplug and replug the board.
  • Wrong board version picked? Just flash again with the right one.
